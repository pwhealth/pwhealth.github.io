About Us

Peaceful Water Health & Fitness is started by Kevin Juliano and Zoe Huang in 2013.

We provide health care based on Traditional Chinese Medicine; fitness training based on the traditional martial arts, and cultural enrichment that promotes calmness and relaxation.

Our space

Our bright, airy space supports healing, balance, and relaxation.

Meet your practitioner

Kevin Juliano

Kevin Juliano has been studying and practicing Chinese medicine since 2004. He earned diplomas in acupuncture, Chinese herbal medicine, and Doctor of Traditional Chinese Medicine from the Toronto School of Traditional Chinese Medicine, as well as Masters of Science in Oriental Medicine from the New York College of Traditional Chinese Medicine. Kevin also studied Chinese language and culture in Taipei, Taiwan, and has interned in China at hospitals in the cities of Shanghai, Jinan, and Beijing. In addition to practicing Chinese medicine, Kevin was also an instructor at the Toronto School of Traditional Chinese Medicine, where he has taught Chinese Medical Theory, Tuina Massage, Chinese Herbal Medicine, Clinical training, and Taijiquan and Qigong.

Zoe Huang

Zoe has studied Taijiqaun for over 20 years and has over 10 years of teaching experience. Zoe was born in Taipei, Taiwan and immigrated to Toronto, Canada with her family in 1994. She met Kevin Juliano in Toronto while studying at the Rising Sun School of T'ai Chi Ch'uan.
